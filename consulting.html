Environmental Consulting

EcOz Environmental Consultants – Darwin – NT (Australia)

Providing environmental surveys, planning, and statutory documents for the Australian and Northern Territories Governments and mining companies in the Tropical and Arid zones of the Australian Northern Territory. Tasks included the provision of Environmental Impact Assessments and Management Plans, Rehabilitation and Weed Monitoring, and water sampling. These required a range of skills such as report writing, mapping and editing satellite and airborne imagery, fauna and flora surveys, erosion assessment and remediation.

Low Ecological Services, Alice Springs – NT (Australia)

Performing a wide range of environmental surveys to pastoral properties, mining and pipeline companies in central Australia (NT and WA). Tasks included the provision of Environmental Impact Assessments and Management Plans, Rehabilitation and Weed Monitoring, Land Clearing Applications, and water sampling. These required a range of skills such as report writing, mapping and editing satellite and airborne imagery, fauna and flora surveys, erosion assessment and ecosystem function analysis.
